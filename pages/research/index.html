Research

Digital Agriculture & Remote Sensing Research

My research focuses on developing innovative solutions for precision agriculture through advanced remote sensing technologies, machine learning, and IoT systems. I work on detecting crop diseases, monitoring plant health, and optimizing agricultural practices using cutting-edge technology from leaf-level to large-scale agricultural monitoring.

Remote Sensing & Hyperspectral Imaging

Utilizing drone-based RGB, multispectral, hyperspectral, and LiDAR sensors alongside satellite imagery (Sentinel-2, Landsat-9, ECOSTRESS, EMIT) for comprehensive agricultural monitoring.

Hyperspectral Imaging Drone Technology Satellite Imagery LiDAR

Plant Disease Detection

Early detection of parasitic weeds and plant diseases using advanced imaging techniques and deep learning models for timely intervention and crop protection.

Disease Detection Parasitic Weeds Early Warning Crop Protection

Machine Learning & AI

Developing deep learning models and machine learning algorithms to extract insights from diverse agricultural data sources and automate decision-making processes.

Deep Learning Computer Vision Neural Networks Data Analytics

IoT & Smart Agriculture

Implementing Internet of Things systems and sensor networks in farms for real-time data collection, environmental monitoring, and precision agriculture applications.

IoT Systems Sensor Networks Real-time Monitoring Smart Farming

Wildfire Monitoring

Using satellite imagery and deep learning techniques for wildfire detection and monitoring to support emergency response and agricultural protection.

Wildfire Detection Emergency Response Satellite Monitoring Risk Assessment

Precision Phenotyping

High-throughput phenotyping systems for stress detection, growth monitoring, and trait analysis to support plant breeding and crop improvement programs.

Phenotyping Stress Detection Plant Breeding Trait Analysis

Current Research Projects

Branched Broomrape Detection in Tomato

Ongoing - PhD Thesis

Developing advanced remote sensing techniques for early detection of branched broomrape parasitic weeds in tomato fields using drone-based multispectral imaging and deep learning algorithms.

3D Radiative Transfer Modeling

Research Collaboration

Creating sophisticated models for specialty crops to understand light interaction with plant canopies for improved remote sensing accuracy and agricultural insights.

Smart Greenhouse Systems

Completed

Designed and manufactured an experimental smart greenhouse integrating IoT systems and machine learning for high-throughput stress phenotyping and disease detection.

Agricultural Vision Competition

Completed

Developed semantic segmentation models using convolutional neural networks for the Agricultural Vision 2021 Competition focusing on automated crop analysis.
